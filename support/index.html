Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I move to a new phone? Settings › Your data › Export my data, then keep the file somewhere safe. The app doesn't import yet.

Apple Health shows no data. Check Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Hormesis Companion and allow the types you want.

Important

Hormesis Companion supports general wellness habits. It doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
