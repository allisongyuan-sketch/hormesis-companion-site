Hormesis Companion Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Hormesis Companion is a wellness app that runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your information. This policy explains what the app keeps on your device and how you control it.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps your check-ins, logs, goals, settings and its daily guidance in a private, encrypted store on your device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code, and it makes no network connections.

The app's data is excluded from iCloud and device backups, so it stays on the device where you entered it.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the app reads only the types you allow, such as sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, breathing rate, wrist temperature, steps and workouts. It uses them on your device to compare each day with your own usual. The app does not write to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can change or remove access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

Notifications

Reminders and timer alerts are scheduled on your device only, and only if you turn them on.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings › Your data › Export my data creates a file you can save or share. The app deletes its temporary copy when you close the share sheet. What happens to the file afterwards is up to you.
  • Delete: Settings › Your data › Delete all data removes everything the app stored. Deleting the app does the same.
  • Keep records: you can have records older than one or two years deleted automatically.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
